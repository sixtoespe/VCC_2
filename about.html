About Us

The Diamond City Sluggers are a community baseball club built on teamwork, fair play, and a love of the game.

Our Mission

To give players of every age a place to learn, compete, and have fun.

Our History

  • 1985: Club founded by a group of local friends.
  • 1998: Won our first league championship.
  • 2012: Moved to our new home at Riverside Park.
  • 2024: Launched the youth baseball academy.
